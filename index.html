About

I am a PhD candidate in Pharmacy at the School of Life Sciences, Nanjing University. My research sits at the intersection of computational biology, medical artificial intelligence and precision oncology. I aim to develop interpretable deep learning frameworks to untangle tumor microenvironment heterogeneity, identify clinically actionable molecular biomarkers, and advance AI‑assisted patient stratification and therapeutic response prediction.

During my master’s training, I focused on pangenomics, transposable elements, and population‑scale genomic analysis, with multiple peer‑reviewed publications and a public pangenome database for peanut. Now I extend these computational toolkits into human cancer multi‑omics, bridging basic genomic discovery and translational pharmaceutical research.

Research Interests

  • Interpretable Graph Learning for Cancer Precision Subtyping: Build masked multi‑omics graph transformers to integrate transcriptomic, genomic and clinical data; dissect immune ecosystem stratification in MSS/pMMR colorectal cancer for immunotherapy patient selection.
  • Multi‑Omics Foundation Model & Representation Learning: Develop biologically informed deep learning to fuse bulk, single‑cell and spatial omics data; extract robust disease signatures while retaining model interpretability for clinical trust.
  • Pangenomics & Structural Variation Biology: Characterize repetitive elements, transposon dynamics and subgenome asymmetry; build computational pipelines for graph pangenome and population genomic variation mining.
  • AI‑Driven Translational Pharmacology: Biomarker discovery, survival risk modeling, computational drug response prediction, and bioinformatics database construction for omics data sharing.
